Short browser-based learning bites for Track A, Authentic & Inclusive Leadership.

Live at `https://dimensionsgroup.github.io/GACA-Learning-Bites-SDP-/`. QR codes for each bite are in `qr/`.

## How each bite works
- **Five Pillars (Reinforce):** learn the five pillars, choose the hardest, get a key message and a practice activity.
- **Ladder of Inference (Apply):** a short case, three questions with instant feedback, and a question to carry based on the score.
- **One sentence (Commit):** the participant completes a commitment sentence, then adds a 30-day calendar reminder, emails it to themselves or copies it. Opening the page again after 30 days shows the commitment back with "هل التزمتَ به؟".

## Privacy
Nothing is sent anywhere. Choices, scores and commitments stay in the participant's own browser only.
